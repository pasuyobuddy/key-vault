KeyVault – Privacy Policy

App by PasuyoLabs (Antonio De Guzman) · Effective date: 18 October 2026 (app version 1.2.0)

KeyVault is a password manager. This policy explains what information the app ("KeyVault") handles and why. The short version: your passwords are encrypted and stay on your phone. We never see them.

1. Your passwords stay on your device, encrypted

Everything you save in KeyVault (platform names, usernames, passwords, websites and notes) is encrypted on your device with AES-256 using a key made from your master password. The encrypted vault is stored only on your phone.

We have no server. KeyVault never uploads your vault, your master password, or anything inside it. We cannot read, recover or reset your data.

When you create your vault, the app shows you a recovery code. Only you have it. If you forget your master password, the recovery code lets you set a new one. If you lose both the master password and the recovery code, nobody can recover your saved passwords.

The app has no sign-up and no account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

2. Backup files (Download, Upload to Drive, Import keys)

Download saves an encrypted backup file (.kvault) to your phone's Documents folder. Upload to Drive opens your phone's share menu so you can send that same encrypted file to Google Drive or any other place you choose; KeyVault itself does not connect to your Google account. Import keys lets you pick a backup file you saved before. Backup files are created only when you ask, and they can only be opened with the master password that was in use when the file was made.

3. Clipboard

When you tap copy, the username or password is placed on your phone's clipboard so you can paste it. Copied passwords are cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds while the app is open.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet – only to load ads (see below). Your vault is never sent over the internet.
  • Storage (Android 10 and older only) – to save your backup file into the Documents folder when you tap Download.
  • Seeing which apps are installed (apps with a home-screen icon) – so you can pick an app with “Choose from my apps” and so the Open button can launch it. This list is only read on your phone, only when you open that screen, and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Fingerprint – only if you turn on fingerprint unlock. Your fingerprint never leaves Android: Android only tells KeyVault whether it matched. KeyVault keeps the vault key locked in Android’s secure Keystore, which only releases it after a fingerprint match.

4b. Autofill (filling logins in other apps)

If you choose KeyVault as your phone’s Autofill service, Android tells KeyVault when you tap a login box in another app or website, and which app or website it is, so KeyVault can suggest the matching saved login. KeyVault only fills a login after you unlock it with your master password or fingerprint. KeyVault does not read the rest of the other app’s screen, does not store what you type there, and does not send anything anywhere. To keep the Autofill service working, KeyVault keeps an encrypted copy of your vault inside its own private app storage; it can only be opened with your master password or fingerprint.

4a. The Open button

Open copies the password to your clipboard and then opens that platform's app or website, so you can paste it. KeyVault does not type into other apps and does not see what you do in them.

KeyVault also blocks screenshots and screen recording of its own screens, to protect your passwords.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are never shown on the lock screen and ad software cannot read the contents of your vault. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (for example phone model, Android version, language),
  • your approximate location based on IP address,
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped).

This information is collected and handled by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message so you can choose how your data is used for ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app, and your vault is erased from your phone.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information. Apart from the ad information collected by Google as described above, the app does not share any data with anyone.

9. Security

Your vault is protected by AES-256-GCM encryption with a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). Choose a long master password that you do not use anywhere else, and keep your phone's screen lock on.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
